AllinSimple Check

2026

AllinSimple Check is a single-user local prototype for manually checking one fixed browser flow against its bundled demo site. It signs into the demo, submits a sample string, and checks the result text.

What it does

  • Signs in, submits a sample, and checks the returned text in a fixed demo flow
  • Offers success, mismatch, and delay modes in the bundled demo
  • Runs manually, one at a time, without automatic retries
  • Keeps run history locally

Sibling projects

AllinSimple Pulse monitors uptime and heartbeats. AllinSimple Desk organizes project operations in a local ledger.
