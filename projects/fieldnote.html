Fieldnote

November 2026 · Retired

Fieldnote was a personal sports research app for keeping analysis and decision history in one continuous workspace. Each sport had a persistent thread, with Markdown handoffs to carry forward prior context.

What it did

  • Ran background research and presented findings in structured analysis tables
  • Kept recommendations separate from bets the user had manually confirmed
  • Preserved conversations and operating criteria across sessions

Why it retired

Interrupted runs and missing or inconsistent table results made repeat checks and manual recovery too common. Even completed runs could leave a result that was incomplete or difficult to use.

What I learned

Measure published, usable output separately from model completion. Reliability includes the full path from a request to a complete result that a person can review.

Tech stack

Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase Postgres, and a separate background worker.
